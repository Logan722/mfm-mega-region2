Family

From barren to a father of twins.

About four years ago I married my wife, and it was a huge struggle — more than five pregnancy losses, including an ectopic and a baby lost in 2020 in the hospital. I kept asking ‘Why me?’ I told my wife, ‘No more — let us adopt.’ She said, ‘Let’s try one more time.’ In January 2021 she conceived; at the first ultrasound they saw nothing and the doctor said we had bad luck. We thanked God anyway. After several Prophetic Sundays, right after Power Must Change Hands, she conceived again — carrying multiples. The doctors wanted us to reduce it to lower the risk; we refused. God took us through, and today I am a father of two boys, to His glory.

Deliverance

A dream that saved my life.

I’m a nurse. Two years ago I had a brain aneurysm on a Saturday and was back at work Monday — God kept His hand over me. Recently, before a trip, I woke around 3:30 from a dream: I was boarding a plane, it was going down, and I kept seeing rushing water. I called my friends already in Cabo and said, ‘God gave me a word — I’m cancelling this flight.’ Two hours later a Category-3 hurricane flooded that hotel from the first to the third floor. If I hadn’t listened, I’d have been stuck in it. I am a living testimony.
