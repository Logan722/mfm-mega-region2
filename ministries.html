Our Ministries

Mega Region 2 is more than weekly services — it is a family with a place for everyone. Explore the arms of ministry raising believers to pray, grow, and serve.

Women Foundation

Empowering Women in Prayer & Purpose

Led by Pastor (Mrs.) Oluwatoyin Oni · President, Mega Region 2 USA

The MFM Women Foundation empowers women through prayer, fellowship, and the Word of God. Through monthly meetings, retreats, and conferences, women are strengthened and equipped for their God-ordained destiny — exemplifying the strength, dignity, and wisdom of Proverbs 31.

Explore Women Foundation →

Gen218

Christian Singles, Rooted in Purpose

Genesis 2:18 · Christian singles ministry

Gen218 equips Christian singles for spiritual growth, wholeness, purpose, and marriage preparation. It is a community where single young adults pursue God’s best — growing in the Word, in prayer, and in godly relationships as they step into their calling.

Explore Gen218 →

Youth Church

A Generation That Carries Fire

Mega Region 2 Youth & Young Adult Church · Houston, TX

The Mega Region 2 Youth Church is a vibrant community of young believers growing in faith and worship. Rooted in Christ and strengthened through prayer, it raises up young people equipped to live victorious, fire-filled lives. The Youth Church has its own home online and gathers in Houston.
