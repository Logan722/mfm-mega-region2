Wednesday declaration · Wednesday, November 25

“The LORD hath done great things for us; whereof we are glad.”Psalm 126:3
  1. The Lord has done great things for me, and greater things are coming.
  2. Every captivity that held my joy this year is turned back today, in Jesus' name.
  3. I sowed in tears, but I reap in joy before this year ends.
