Declaration of the week · Monday, November 23

“Then shall the earth yield her increase; and God, even our own God, shall bless us.”Psalm 67:6
This week, your harvest comes in.As you give thanks this week, the Lord turns your gratitude into increase. Every field you sowed in tears yields its harvest, every delayed blessing arrives, and God, even your own God, blesses you and your household, in Jesus' name. Amen.
