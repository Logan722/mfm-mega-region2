Thursday declaration · Thursday, November 26

“O give thanks unto the LORD; for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever.”Psalm 136:1
  1. The mercy of God over my life and my family endures today and for ever.
  2. On this Thanksgiving Day, every mouth speaking evil against my home is silenced.
  3. I give thanks with my whole heart, and my table shall not lack.
