Friday declaration · Friday, November 27

“The LORD is my strength and my shield; my heart trusted in him, and I am helped: therefore my heart greatly rejoiceth; and with my song will I praise him.”Psalm 28:7
  1. The Lord is my strength and my shield in every place I go today.
  2. Because I trust in Him, I am helped; I will not be stranded.
  3. My heart rejoices, and my song of praise rises above every trouble.
