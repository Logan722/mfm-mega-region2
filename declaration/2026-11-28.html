Saturday declaration · Saturday, November 28

“Thou crownest the year with thy goodness; and thy paths drop fatness.”Psalm 65:11
  1. The Lord crowns the rest of my year with His goodness.
  2. Every path I walk this December drops fatness and favour.
  3. No evil report will close my year; I finish with a crown, in Jesus' name.
