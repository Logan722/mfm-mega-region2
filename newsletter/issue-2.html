The fire didn’t stay in Burlington

Two crusades behind us. Two altars two Saturdays out. Prayer Battle 7 closes the fifteenth day.

A word from the pastor

What God did in September, He is not finished with

Beloved, September was a season of long altars. Prayer Battle 7 has been running since August. The G.O. brought the fire to Burlington on the sixteenth and seventeenth. He carried it to Tampa on the nineteenth and twentieth. Deliverance came out of Mount Zion, and holiness came with it. To every man, woman, and child who prayed at home, who fasted, who travelled — God saw you.

Now He turns us toward October. Two altars are being raised on the same Saturday. The women will gather to rekindle theirs at Prayer City. The singles will gather to align theirs on the same grounds. Whichever one has your name on it — prepare. The fire that fell in Burlington and Tampa did not stay there. It is looking for another altar.

— Pastor Olumide Oni

Prayer points

Fifteen days to close Prayer Battle 7

“But upon mount Zion shall be deliverance, and there shall be holiness.” — Obadiah 1:17

1.   Father, seal every deliverance received at Burlington and at Tampa — let no unclean spirit return to any life You set free, in the name of Jesus.
2.   Every altar of hindrance working against the women of Mega Region 2 — scatter by fire before October 10, in the name of Jesus. (Jer 23:29)
3.   Every singleness born of an evil pattern — O God arise, break the yoke, and align Your sons and daughters at Gen218, in the name of Jesus.
4.   Prepare Tennessee and Texas for what You are about to do at Nashville and at Dallas — go before us as You did in the season past, in the name of Jesus.
5.   O Lord, close Prayer Battle 7 with a testimony in every household that entered it — let no one leave this altar empty-handed, in the name of Jesus. (Deut 4:11)
