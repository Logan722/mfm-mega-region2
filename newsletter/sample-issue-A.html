A word from the pastor

A month for prayer eagles

Beloved of God, August is a training month. The 70-Day Prayer & Fasting programme our General Overseer commissioned — Prayer Battle 7 — is at its midpoint, and the season it is preparing us for is bigger than any one of us. Two Deliverance Crusades under one anointing. Regional gatherings for the women and for Gen218. In every one of them, the Lord is calling us to be prayer eagles.

This month, print the prayer points below. Pray them at midnight. Come with a friend to the Zonal Women's Conference in Duluth on Saturday. Book your travel now for North Carolina and Florida. The fire in the bone (Jeremiah 20:9) cannot be shut in.

— Pastor Olumide Oni
Principal Regional Overseer, Mega Region 2 USA

This month, we pray

The fire in the bone cannot be shut in

“…his word was in mine heart as a burning fire shut up in my bones, and I was weary with forbearing, and I could not stay.” — Jeremiah 20:9

  1. Every fire the enemy set in my life this year — receive the water of the Holy Ghost and die, in the name of Jesus.
  2. O God, arise and let the crusades at Burlington and Tampa become historic altars of deliverance in this region, in the name of Jesus.
  3. Every household evil pattern following my family — I break your back with the hammer of the word of God, in the name of Jesus.
  4. Anointing for prevailing prayer — like Elijah on Mount Carmel, fall upon me and my household this month, in the name of Jesus.
  5. O Lord, possess my possessions — spiritual, financial, marital, ministerial — and let every enemy holding them go, in the name of Jesus.
