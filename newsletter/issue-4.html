A WORD FROM THE PASTOR

Keep the fire burning.

Beloved,

Something shifted at the Women's Retreat. The altar burned. Voices rose. Chains fell. If you were there, you felt it — the Spirit of God moved with a weight that could not be mistaken. If you heard about it after, you still carry the overflow, because what God does in one room, He releases over the whole region.

But fire does not keep itself. The retreat is behind us; what matters now is what we carry forward. The Word that cut through on Saturday has to keep cutting through on Monday morning. The prayers that broke open the heavens over the Banquet Hall have to keep rising over your home, your workplace, your children. The anointing that fell is yours to steward, not just remember.

Our Mega Crusades with Dr. D.K. Olukoya are weeks away — Tennessee on November 19, Dallas on November 21. These are not events to attend. These are altars to carry. Come expectant. Come hungry. Come with the ones who need to be in the room. The fire has not grown cold.

Pastor Olumide Oni
Principal Regional Overseer · Mega Region 2 USA

PRAYER POINTS

Pray these this week.

1

Carry the fire. That every anointing received at the Women's Retreat shall not go cold in my home, in Jesus' name.
2

Stand with the crusades. That the fire of God shall fall in Tennessee on November 19 and in Dallas on November 21, and no device of the enemy shall prosper against these gatherings.
3

Pray for your household. That every member of my family shall encounter the God of Mount Zion this week, and that no spiritual blockage shall remain standing in our home.
4

Bring someone with you. That the Lord shall put on my heart the name of one person who must be in the room at Tennessee or Dallas, and that I shall extend the invitation this week.
