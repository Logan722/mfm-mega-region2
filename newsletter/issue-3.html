A word from the pastor

Testimonies are not the end — they are the invitation

Beloved, Burlington is behind us. Tampa is behind us. The sciatica left. The chronic pain left. Men and women who came carrying one thing walked out carrying something else. Thank God. But testimony is never the end of the matter — it is an invitation to a bigger altar.

On Saturday, October 10, two altars will burn at Prayer City on the same day. The women will gather in the Banquet Hall to rekindle theirs. The singles will gather on the same grounds for divine alignment. Four days from the moment you read this. Then on Saturday, October 11, Prayer Battle 7 closes — seventy days of fasting and warfare come to their appointed end. Finish well.

If you have been standing afar off, come near. If you have been coming near, come closer. The fire has not grown cold.

— Pastor Olumide Oni

Prayer points

Five days to finish Prayer Battle 7

“And it shall come to pass, that whosoever shall call on the name of the LORD shall be delivered.” — Joel 2:32

1
 Father, every yoke You began to lift in Burlington and in Tampa — complete the work this week in every life, in the name of Jesus.
2
 O God arise, let the two altars of October 10 become one fire that no evil power can quench, in the name of Jesus.
3
 Every weariness attacking the saints as Prayer Battle 7 closes — receive the strength of the Holy Ghost, in the name of Jesus. (Isaiah 40:31)
4
 Prepare Tennessee and Texas for Nashville and Dallas — let no hostile altar stand against Your gathering, in the name of Jesus. (Jer 23:29)
5
 O Lord, let every testimony born in September bear fruit in a hundredfold harvest before the year closes, in the name of Jesus. (Obadiah 1:17)
