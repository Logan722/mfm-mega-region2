A WORD FROM THE PASTOR

Not events. Altars.

Beloved,

In eleven days, Dr. D.K. Olukoya — General Overseer of Mountain of Fire and Miracles Ministries worldwide — stands before the altar in Tennessee. Two days later, he stands in Dallas. The man God is using to shake the nations with the sword of the Spirit will be in two cities we can drive to, sit in, bring our burdens to.

These are not events to attend. These are altars to carry. The arena will be full of people who came expecting the fire to fall. Many will leave different than they walked in. Chains will break. Wombs will open. Diagnoses will be reversed. Testimonies that have been locked up for years will finally get their release.

But the God who shows up at a crusade shows up because people came hungry. He honors expectation. And He honors the people who refuse to come alone — who bring the sister who hasn't been to church in two years, the brother who's been struggling in silence, the neighbor who keeps saying "maybe one day."

So I'll ask you plainly: who are you bringing to Nashville or Dallas? Hit reply and tell me one name you're praying to bring. I'll pray with you for that name every day until the altar is set.

Pastor Olumide Oni
Principal Regional Overseer · Mega Region 2 USA

PRAYER POINTS

Pray these until the altar is set.

1

For the General’s coming. That the Lord shall carry Dr. D.K. Olukoya in strength and anointing to Tennessee and to Dallas, and that every word out of his mouth shall accomplish the purpose for which it is sent.
2

For the one I am bringing. Call out their name before the Lord. That God shall soften every resistance, open every door, remove every excuse, and bring them into the room.
3

For the altar itself. That no weapon formed against these gatherings shall prosper. That weather, logistics, traffic, and the accuser’s whisper shall not hinder the fire of God from falling.
4

For my own hungry heart. That I shall not come as a spectator but as a son or daughter crying for more. That when the Word goes forth, something in me shall never be the same again.
