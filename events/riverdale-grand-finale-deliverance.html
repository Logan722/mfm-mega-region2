Grand Finale Deliverance Service — MFM Riverdale

Sunday, November 29, 2026 · 8:00 AM ET

The God That Astonishes His People (Mark 7:37)

WhenSunday, November 29, 2026 · 8:00 AM ET
WhereMFM Riverdale — Bombay Plaza, 6989 GA-85, Suite D1, Riverdale, GA 30274
MinisteringDr. D.K. Olukoya (G.O., MFM Worldwide) · Pastor Olumide Oni (Mega Region 2 USA PRO) · anointed men and women of God
HostPastor Francis Benede

MFM Riverdale, Atlanta invites you to the Grand Finale of its quarterly Deliverance Service — the last of the year. Theme: The God that astonishes His people (Mark 7:37). It is a fasting programme: bring a bottle of oil, and bring any bill or note as a point of contact to be prayed over and returned to you for financial blessing. Sunday, November 29 at 8:00 AM (Eastern Time). Invite someone!
