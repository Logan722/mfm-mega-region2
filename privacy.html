Privacy Policy

Effective: August 11, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Mountain of Fire and Miracles Ministries, Mega Region 2 USA ("MFM Mega Region 2," "we," "us") collects when you use our website or subscribe to our newsletter, and how we handle it.

What we collect

  • Information you give us through forms — your name, email address, and (optionally) phone number or country.
  • Basic technical data your browser sends automatically — IP address, device type, pages visited.
  • Engagement data from our newsletter — whether emails were opened and links clicked.

How we use it

  • To send you the newsletter and ministry updates you signed up for.
  • To respond to messages, prayer requests, and enquiries you send us.
  • To help you find the MFM branch nearest you.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information and do not share it with advertisers. We use a small number of trusted service providers (for hosting, email delivery, and analytics) that process data on our behalf under their own privacy commitments. We only disclose personal information to authorities if required by law or to protect someone's safety.

Your rights

  • Unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of any email.
  • Ask what data we hold about you, correct it, or request that we delete it.
  • Withdraw consent for us to contact you.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@mfmmegaregion2usa.org.

Cookies

The website uses a small number of essential and analytics cookies to keep the site working and help us understand which content is useful. You can clear or block cookies through your browser at any time.

Children

The website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental consent.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent change.
