1. Who we are

This website (mfmmegaregion2usa.org) is operated by Mountain of Fire and Miracles Ministries — Mega Region 2 USA ("MFM Mega Region 2," "we," "us," or "our"), a regional ministry of Mountain of Fire and Miracles Ministries with headquarters at MFM USA Prayer City, 10000 Kleckley Dr, Houston, TX 77075.

This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

When you fill in a form on this site — the newsletter signup, contact form, prayer request form, or event RSVP — you may share:

  • Name (first and last)
  • Email address
  • Phone number (only if you choose to provide it)
  • Country or city (for regional segmentation)
  • Prayer request text, testimony, or message content you type into a form

Information collected automatically

When you visit the site, our hosting and analytics tools automatically log routine technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and the site you came from. This is standard web-server activity used to keep the site running and detect abuse.

Information from emails we send

If you subscribe to our newsletter, our email provider (Brevo) records whether you open our emails and click links inside them. This helps us understand which topics are useful and improve future issues.

3. How we use it

We use the information above to:

  • Send you the newsletter, event updates, and ministry announcements you signed up for.
  • Respond to messages, prayer requests, and event enquiries you send us.
  • Connect you with the branch of MFM Mega Region 2 nearest to you when you request it.
  • Keep the site secure and working properly.
  • Comply with legal obligations (e.g., recordkeeping for anti-spam laws).

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not use it for profiling unrelated to ministry communications.

4. Legal basis (for visitors in the UK / EU / EEA)

If GDPR or UK GDPR applies to you, we process your personal data on these bases:

  • Consent — you ticked a box to subscribe to the newsletter or submitted a form knowingly. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interest — running the website securely, replying to your enquiries, and communicating about the ministry you engaged with.
  • Legal obligation — retaining records where required by law.

5. Third-party processors

We use these service providers, who process personal data on our behalf under their own privacy commitments:

  • Brevo (Sendinblue SA) — email newsletter delivery, contact list storage, and analytics. Data is stored in the European Union. Privacy policy: brevo.com/legal/privacypolicy.
  • Netlify — website hosting and form submission processing. Privacy policy: netlify.com/privacy.
  • Google Fonts & YouTube — page fonts and embedded sermon videos. When you view a page or watch a video, Google may receive your IP address and browser info. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Eventbrite — used for public event registrations we link to. If you register for an event, your data is handled under Eventbrite's policy: eventbrite.com/l/legalterms.

We do not share your information with any other party except where required by law (e.g., a valid court order) or to protect the safety of a person.

6. Cookies

This site uses a small number of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary — remember your consent choices and keep forms working. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics / measurement — anonymous counts of page views to see which content is useful. No advertising cookies.
  • Third-party embeds — if you play a YouTube video, YouTube may set cookies. This is under Google's control, not ours.

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time.

7. How long we keep it

  • Newsletter subscribers: until you unsubscribe. Unsubscribed contacts are kept for up to 12 months in a suppression list to make sure we don't accidentally email you again, then deleted.
  • Prayer requests / messages: up to 24 months, then deleted, unless you ask for follow-up ministry care and we mutually keep the record longer.
  • Server logs: up to 90 days.

8. Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • Unsubscribe — click the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any newsletter, or email us at the address below.
  • Access — ask what data we hold about you.
  • Correct — ask us to fix inaccurate information.
  • Delete — ask us to erase your data, subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Object — ask us to stop processing your data for a particular purpose.
  • Complain — lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (e.g., the UK ICO or your EU country's supervisory authority) if you believe we've handled your data unlawfully.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@mfmmegaregion2usa.org. We'll respond within 30 days.

9. Children

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the UK / EEA) without parental consent. If you believe we've collected such information, please contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Security

We use HTTPS across the site, keep credentials in secure environment variables, and rely on reputable providers (Brevo, Netlify) that meet industry security standards. No system is perfect — if we ever become aware of a breach that affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of the page will always reflect the most recent change. Substantive changes will be announced in the newsletter or on the site.
