FIRE FOR THE WEEK

Fire for the Harvest

Week of December 1, 2026
"Then shall the earth yield her increase; and God, even our own God, shall bless us."
PSALM 67:6
33 prayer points for the week · new set every Monday
📄 Download PDF Share on WhatsApp

Consecration

  1. Lord, I present my body as a living sacrifice this week — holy and acceptable, in the name of Jesus. (Rom 12:1)
  2. I set apart these seven days for You — my time, my words, my attention. Receive them, in Jesus' name.
  3. Let every area of my life be consecrated to Your harvest this week, in the name of Jesus.
  4. O Lord, mark me with Your fire — let men know I have been with You, in Jesus' name.
  5. I decrease; let Jesus increase in me this week, in the name of Jesus. (John 3:30)

Personal Cleansing

  1. Every hidden sin in my life, I renounce you and cover you with the blood of Jesus.
  2. Holy Spirit, search me and reveal every secret thing that must go, in Jesus' name. (Ps 139:23)
  3. Let the fire of God burn out every root of bitterness, pride and compromise in me, in Jesus' name.
  4. O Lord, create in me a clean heart; renew a right spirit within me. (Ps 51:10)
  5. I close every door I have opened to the devil in ignorance, in the name of Jesus.

Breaking Every Yoke

  1. Every yoke of my father's house and my mother's house, break by the blood of Jesus. (Isa 10:27)
  2. Every generational curse of poverty, delay or barrenness — be nullified, in Jesus' name.
  3. Every evil pattern repeating in my family line, stop at my generation, in the name of Jesus.
  4. I possess my possessions today — nothing withheld, nothing delayed. (Obadiah 1:17)

Silencing Enemy Altars

  1. Every evil altar speaking against my destiny, be silenced by the blood of Jesus.
  2. Every power monitoring my progress, be struck blind, in the name of Jesus.
  3. Every arrow fired at me from darkness, go back to the sender, in Jesus' name. (Ps 7:16)
  4. Every strongman assigned to my advancement, be bound now, in Jesus' name. (Matt 12:29)

Healing and Wholeness

  1. Every sickness in my body, I command you to flee now, in the name of Jesus. (Mark 16:18)
  2. Let the healing fire of the Holy Ghost flow through every organ and cell of my body, in Jesus' name.
  3. Every wound in my soul that no one else has reached, O Lord, touch it today, in Jesus' name.
  4. Jehovah Rapha, make me whole — nothing missing, nothing broken, in Jesus' name. (Ps 147:3)

The Harvest — Breakthrough & Open Doors

  1. Every door shut against me by the enemy, be opened now, in the name of Jesus. (Rev 3:8)
  2. Every seed I have sown in tears, yield your harvest this week, in Jesus' name. (Ps 126:5-6)
  3. Every delayed blessing attached to my name, be released today, in Jesus' name.
  4. The mountain of _______________ in my life — BE REMOVED, in the name of Jesus. (Mark 11:23)
  5. Every prayer on hold in the heavens, be released this week, in Jesus' name.
  6. Then shall the earth yield her increase — my harvest comes in, in the name of Jesus. (Ps 67:6)

Carrying the Fire

  1. Let the fire of the Lord cover every member of my household today, in Jesus' name.
  2. My children shall not be lost to the world, in the name of Jesus. (Isa 54:13)
  3. Let every demonic assignment against my spouse be cancelled, in the name of Jesus.
  4. Make me a carrier of Your presence — in my home, workplace and city, in Jesus' name.
  5. What God has begun in me, He will finish — I shall not go back, in Jesus' name. (Phil 1:6)
